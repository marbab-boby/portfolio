About

Space science background, geospatial engineering focus

I'm a GIS Developer with a BS in Space Sciences from the Institute of Space Technology, Islamabad. I work across the geospatial stack: designing PostgreSQL/PostGIS databases, writing backend services in Node.js and Express.js, publishing layers through GeoServer, and building interactive maps with Leaflet.

My academic work in remote sensing and climatology (Landsat and MODIS imagery, ERA reanalysis, ETCCDI climate indices) means I understand the data behind the maps, not only the tools that display it. I also enjoy automating repetitive office work with small, dependable tools.
